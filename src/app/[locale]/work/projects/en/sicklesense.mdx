## Overview

This project was created for a month long hackathon hosted by [AfroTech](https://afrotech.com/) and [Blavity](https://blavity.com/). The goal was to develop a solution that leverages AI to address a pressing issue in the Black community. 

I decided to focus on Sickle Cell Disease, a condition that affects millions of people worldwide, including me.

I ended up winning [2nd place and $5000](https://devpost.com/software/sicklesense) for this project, so I'm pretty proud of it!

## Inspiration

A lot of people don't expect this, but managing Sickle Cell disease involves more than just the physical symptoms.

Patients often feel misunderstood by healthcare providers and lack a supportive community. Living with SCD myself, I’ve experienced these challenges firsthand.

This inspired me to build SickleSense, using both qualitative and quantitative data to help patients manage their condition more effectively, empowering them to live better lives.

## Key Features

### AI-Powered Health Plans

- Users can create and customize health plans based on their real-time health data.
- AI-generated recommendations suggest lifestyle changes, medication adjustments, and more.
- Health plans can be updated, paused, or deleted as needed.

### Comprehensive Health Logging

- Users can log medications, symptoms, and appointments with reminders via email or SMS.
- In-app notes enable users to document health trends, which improves communication during medical consultations.
- Building a comprehensive health history enables more accurate AI insights and personal care improvements.

### Wearable Device Integration

- SickleSense integrates with Fitbit to monitor heart rate, oxygen levels, and activity in real-time.
- Data from Fitbit is used to power AI predictions, helping users stay ahead of potential health crises with timely recommendations.

### AI-Powered Chatbot

- The AI chatbot provides context-aware health advice based on a user’s personal data.
- Using Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) and a database of 54,000 SCD-related documents, the chatbot offers accurate responses and health insights.

### Community Forum

- Users can share experiences, ask questions, and provide support to others in a dedicated community forum.
- This fosters a sense of belonging, reducing isolation that many patients with chronic illnesses face.

### Customizable Dashboard & Alerts

- Users can customize their dashboard to focus on their most important health metrics.
- Automated email and SMS reminders for medications, appointments, and health updates are available.
- Real-time alerts notify users of potential crises based on their health data and Fitbit metrics.

## Tech Stack

- **Next.js**: I used this powerful front-end framework to build the user interface.
- **OpenAI & Google Gemini**: I integrated these AI tools for health insights and recommendations.
- **Fitbit API**: I utilized this API for real-time health data ingestion from wearable devices.
- **PostgreSQL & Prisma**: I managed the database for user health data and logs using these technologies.
- **Elasticsearch**: I implemented this to provide semantic search and AI-powered insights for the platform.
- **Bun.js**: I used this backend service for real-time data ingestion.
- **Render**: I deployed services efficiently using this cloud platform.
- **Tailwind CSS & shadcn/ui**: I created a responsive and accessible user interface with these tools.

## Challenges and Learnings

One of the toughest challenges was ensuring data privacy and security while handling sensitive health information, even though this was a hackathon project.

Additionally, integrating Fitbit’s real-time data API and syncing this data with AI-powered insights proved to be difficult, especially when making sure that data was processed securely and effectively.

The final major challenge was designing an intuitive user interface that caters to individuals who may be in pain or fatigue. I had to play around with different color schemes, font sizes, and layouts to ensure that it was "just right".
